Privacy Policy: Daily Training Coach

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Daily Training Coach ("the app") is published by Doug Minear (mineargear.com).

What the app stores

The app stores your setup answers (such as age, health check answers, goals and equipment), your workouts, and your morning check-ins (such as sleep hours, heart rate, blood pressure, energy and joint discomfort).

Where it is stored

All of it is stored only on your device. The app has no account, no server, and no analytics, and it does not send your information anywhere.

Files you create

When you tap Back up data or Export to Excel, the app creates a file and hands it to your phone's save or share screen. Where that file goes (your phone, Google Drive, email and so on) is your choice, and is covered by that service's privacy policy.

Links

Exercise video buttons open YouTube searches in your browser. YouTube's privacy policy applies there.

Children

The app is for adults 18 and over.

Phone backups

If your phone's own Google backup is turned on, Android may include the app's saved data in your personal Google account backup so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup belongs to you; the publisher never sees it.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes everything it stored.

Health information

The app gives general fitness guidance, not medical advice. It does not diagnose or treat any condition.

Contact

Questions: contact the publisher through mineargear.com.
